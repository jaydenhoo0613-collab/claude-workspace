FAQ

Questions coaches ask first.

What counts as a "qualified" appointment?

Before launch we agree on the criteria in writing, for example budget, business stage and the problem they want solved. A call is only billed when the prospect meets those criteria and books onto your calendar.

Is ad spend included in the $150?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your own ad account, so you keep full ownership and visibility. The $150 is Resultage's fee per qualified booked call.

Do you take the sales calls?

No. We fill your calendar with qualified prospects, and you or your closers run the calls. That keeps the relationship and the sale in your hands.

How fast will I see calls?

Most of the first month goes into the audit, the funnel build and the ad launch. Calls usually start once ads are live, then volume builds toward 15–30 a month by day 120 as we optimise.

Am I locked into a long contract?

There's no monthly retainer. You pay the setup fee once, then per qualified call. We'll cover the exact terms on your fit call.
